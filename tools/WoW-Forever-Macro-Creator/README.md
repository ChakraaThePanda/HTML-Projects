# WoW-Forever-Macro-Creator
Build WoW Forever macros without memorizing the syntax.

- Combine several spells or items into one button, checked top to bottom.
- Search your class spells as you type, with their icons, and pick a lower rank when you want to downrank. Hover a spell (or tap its icon on a phone) to see its tooltip: cost, range, cast time, cooldown and what it does. The spell list comes from the WoW Forever game files. Your Hearthstone is in the search for every character too. You can still type anything yourself, like items or trinket slots.
- Toggle Shift, Ctrl, Alt or "no modifier key" per spell, pick a target (mouseover, yourself, focus, cursor and more) and add conditions about the target (friendly, enemy, alive, dead) or about you (in combat, stealthed, mounted, swimming, indoors, outdoors and more).
- Pick your race to add its WoW Forever racial abilities to the spell search, with the same tooltips. Races your class can't be in WoW Forever are greyed out, and priest spells only some races get are labelled.
- Spell tooltips match WoW Forever's own, with numbers as a level 60 character sees them.
- Build cast sequences: a purple box with its own list of spells in the order you want, cast one per press. Choose when it starts over (after a number of seconds, on a new target, when leaving combat or when holding Shift, Ctrl or Alt), and get a warning when a spell with a cooldown would hold the sequence up.
- Pick your class to use stances and forms: Warrior stances (tick several for combos like Battle or Berserker), Druid forms, Rogue Stealth, Priest Shadowform and Shaman Ghost Wolf.
- Start the macro with `#showtooltip` (automatic, a specific spell, or following each spell's conditions) or `#show`, and add common extra lines like `/stopcasting`, `/dismount` or `/startattack`.
- Live preview with a 255 character counter. Anything past the limit is struck through so you can see what the game would cut off.
- Plain English summary of what each spell line does, plus warnings for spells that can never be reached.
- Paste any macro, from the game or one you found online, to rebuild it on the page so you can edit it.
- Put a spell on its own line so it runs too, handy for spells that don't trigger the global cooldown.
- Save your macros in the browser. They are grouped by class with their spell icons, and you can put them in your own order.
- Works on phones too, with the character count and copy button kept at the bottom of the screen.
